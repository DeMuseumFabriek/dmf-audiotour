Coin Hoard of Hezingen

Coin Hoard of Hezingen.

A remarkable hoard dating from the 6th and 7th centuries AD has been discovered in the Springendal valley near Hezingen. The find consists of over a hundred gold coins, several silver coins, and jewelry. They date back to the Merovingian period, the early Middle Ages. The objects were found near wooden posts at a crossroads of ancient roads. Valuable possessions were left at this location for about a century. Therefore, archaeologists consider it a sacrificial site. This is not a hidden cache of money. The coins and jewelry were deliberately given as gifts to higher powers. This well-researched site is one of the most important early medieval discoveries in the Netherlands.
