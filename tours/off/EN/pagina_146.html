The Steenwijk Wooden Wheel

The Steenwijk Wooden Wheel.

This wooden wheel was found at the De Eese estate near Steenwijk. It dates from the late Neolithic period, around 2600 BC. It is a large, disk-shaped wooden wheel with a thickened hub through which the axle passed. Wheels enabled cart transport, an important innovation in prehistoric times. Yet, many wheels found in the peat were never used. Some were not even completely finished. This wheel, too, was found in the bog and shows no clear signs of use. Therefore, archaeologists believe it was deliberately left behind. It may have been an offering at a place considered sacred.
