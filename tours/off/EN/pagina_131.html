Hoards of Elsenerbroek and Enterveen

Several hoards from the Bronze Age, dating from around 1000 to 700 BC, have been discovered in Elsenerbroek and nearby Enterveen. These are collections of objects placed together in peat or wet soil. These hoards contained bronze spearheads, bracelets, battle hammers, and even semi-finished products. Some areas were marked with stones. Many objects were barely used. Therefore, archaeologists believe they were votive offerings. These were gifts to gods or higher powers, deliberately and permanently left behind. Some hoards contain remains of raw bronze. Perhaps these were the stockpiles of a bronze smith who never returned.
