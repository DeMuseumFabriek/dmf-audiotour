Introduction – Sacrificial Treasures

Welcome to the exhibition Sacrificial Treasures: Rituals in the Twente Landscape by De Museumfabriek. Long before Christianity, there were sacred natural sites in Twente. People believed that peat bogs, rivers, trees, and hills were enlivened. At such sites, they offered sacrifices to gods and spirits, either to ask for help or to give thanks. Marshes were particularly important. They were dangerous and mysterious and were seen as gateways to another world. Therefore, they were visited for centuries. In this exhibition, you will discover why archaeologists consider these finds to be sacrificial and how ancient rituals later merged with Christianity.
