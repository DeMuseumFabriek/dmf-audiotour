Ball of wool from the peat.

This ball of wool dates back to the Iron Age, around 600 BC. It was found in the raised bog near Roswinkel in southeast Drenthe. The wool was well preserved because it lay in wet, oxygen-poor peat. Organic material hardly decomposes under such conditions. Several balls of wool from the late Bronze Age and early Iron Age have been found in Drenthe. The ball shows that people were already spinning wool for textiles back then. Raised bogs were not inhabited. Therefore, archaeologists believe this is not a lost object, but an offering. It may have been a ritual gift, for example, for fertility or a healthy herd.
