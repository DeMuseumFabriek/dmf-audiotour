###### Welcome 

Welcome to the audio tour “Los Hoes: farm life in Twente” from the Museum Factory!

In this tour we talk about farm life in Twente from about 1100 AD.

You will hear how people lived, what they ate and drank, what their main activities were, and much more.

You can explore Los Hoes via a QR code and your mobile phone. Use earphones, or read the text quietly on your screen.

On your mobile you decide how much you want to hear. A button takes you to the next story. 

The whole story takes about 30 minutes. If you want to know more, you can read or watch additional information, such as photos or videos. Sometimes there is additional background information via the button **“The Atlas of Once”**.

We start about Los Hoes at its **origin and construction**. Later we will tell you, among other things, where the name Los Hoes comes from.

Have fun!
