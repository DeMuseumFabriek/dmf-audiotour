You are now looking at the Twente Painting Gallery. Together with the Oudheidkamer Twente, we have around 200 paintings in our collection. Exactly 100 of them are on display here. The Museumfabriek does not collect art, so the quality of the paintings is not our primary focus. What we find important is the story the paintings tell. For instance, the story of Job Dixon, who played a key role in establishing the first steam-powered spinning mill in Twente.  
On the panels next to the paintings, you will find QR codes that you can scan. Each QR code reveals a different story—sometimes about people, sometimes about landscapes.  
When you have finished viewing the paintings, continue your route through the museum. The next listening point is at the mammoth skeleton.
