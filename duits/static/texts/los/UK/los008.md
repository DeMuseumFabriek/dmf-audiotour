###### Floor

Before we continue with life in Los Hoes, we look at the floor.

The living area was covered with small field boulders.

The floor of the business area (the part) consisted of rammed loam.

When the Los Hoes was installed in the museum (2008), the floor of the section was made of loam from the Roombeek.

However, in wet weather it became a mess, as people walked through the entire museum.

That is why the clay was replaced by concrete of the same color, so that it looks authentic, but is easier to keep clean.
