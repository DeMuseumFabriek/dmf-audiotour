To get to Los Hoes, follow the blue dotted line on the map of the Museumfabriek above. Walk through the sliding doors and turn right toward the stairs leading down. After the tunnel, turn right up the ramp toward Hall 1. Los Hoes is located at the end of Hall 1. Click the right arrow or the red dot (LH) to go to the first listening point. If you’re using a touchscreen (cell phone or tablet), you can also swipe.
