  • Sacrificial treasures

    In this exhibition, we are displaying a variety of artifacts found in the peatlands in and around Twente. These “sacrificial treasures,” which were likely valuable at the time, were probably offered to the gods to win their favor and protect the people from disasters.

    This audio tour is available in Dutch.

    This audio tour is available in English.

    This audio guide is available in German.

  • Los Hoes

    Welcome to the Museumfabriek’s audio tour, “Los Hoes: Farm Life in Twente.” Today, you’ll take a trip back in time. Imagine: it’s the year 1100, and you’re a farmer in Twente. How do you live, what do you eat, and what does your farm look like? You’ll hear and see all that on this tour.

    This audio tour is available in Dutch

    This audio tour is available in English
