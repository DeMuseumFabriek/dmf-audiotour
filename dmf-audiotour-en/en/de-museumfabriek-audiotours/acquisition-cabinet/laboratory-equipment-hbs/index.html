HBS Laboratory Equipment

Laboratory equipment from the HBS in Enschede.

1 2 3

In 1864, the first secondary school opened in Enschede. This secondary school was established with the support of textile manufacturer Van Heek and the liberal leader Thorbecke. The laboratory equipment shown here comes from this school, which would later become known as the Stedelijk Kottenpark.
