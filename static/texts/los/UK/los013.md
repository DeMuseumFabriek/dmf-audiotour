###### Turtledove

It was important again for farmers.

That is why a turtledove often hung in Los Hoes.

Turtledoves respond to air pressure differences; they make a different sound at high or low air pressure.

Even more important: the pigeon warned about carbon monoxide.

This danger mainly arose in winter, when there was an open fire in the house and the doors and windows were kept closed to keep heat in.

Carbon monoxide is produced by incomplete combustion of wood or other fuels.

The turtledove was the first to be affected by the carbon monoxide, so people were warned in time.

This was the last story about Los Hoes.
